## Limits on claims carried into later work

Use the corrected references at the scope they support. The title-matched SMOTE paper examines particular datasets and methods; it does not prove that every medical use of SMOTE is invalid. The resting-heart-rate paper reports cohort/meta-analytic associations; it does not establish that a SHAP attribution is causal. The XAI review concerns clinician trust, which is not direct evidence of how lay users interpret this dashboard. An expert heuristic inspection is not a participant user study.

The original use of Obermeyer (2019) for a specific female-underdiagnosis interpretation needs narrower wording or an appropriate source: that cited study concerns racial bias in a healthcare algorithm. New documentation should describe this project's observed gender-group error rates without importing an unverified causal explanation or a source-specific gender claim.

Cross-study AUC and sensitivity comparisons use different samples, targets, predictors and protocols. Keep them as contextual literature discussion; the present work did not perform a controlled head-to-head replication. A benchmark that favours trees on its studied tabular datasets also does not establish that tree methods universally outperform every deep-learning approach.

## Verification boundary

The 62-entry check used DOI metadata, proceedings, publisher/author records and institutional sources. It checked bibliographic identity rather than every full-text claim. Some current pages differ from historical versions and some direct URLs were inaccessible; those limits apply to the corrections above.
